PRIVACY POLICY
MyMcD Shifts
Effective: 23 September 2026

1. Controller
The developer of MyMcD Shifts ("we") processes data only as described here. Contact: https://github.com/dominik-kaspar-dev/MyMcD-shifts/issues

2. Data we process
- Account identifiers and encrypted credentials (e-mail, password, session cookies) stored locally on your device using Android Keystore.
- Shift schedule data fetched from mymcd.eu / next.mymcd.eu and cached encrypted on your device.
- App settings (language, refresh interval, notification preferences) stored locally.
- Notification permission status as required by Android.

We do NOT collect analytics, advertising IDs, location, contacts, or files. The App does not include third-party analytics or ad SDKs.

3. How data is used
- To log in to your schedule account and display your shifts.
- To refresh data in the background and show the home-screen widget.
- To send local notifications you enable (plan changes, reminders).
- Credentials never leave your device except to authenticate with mymcd.eu / next.mymcd.eu over HTTPS.

4. Sharing
We do not sell or share personal data. Data is transmitted only to the official schedule endpoints over TLS, and as required by law.

5. Retention
Data stays on your device until you log out, remove the account, or uninstall the App. Clearing app data removes all stored credentials and cache.

6. Security
Credentials and shift cache are encrypted with a hardware-backed Android Keystore AES-256-GCM key. No remote server operated by us stores your password.

7. Your rights
You may uninstall the App at any time to remove local data. Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights to access, export, or delete data held by mymcd.eu — contact that service for such requests.

8. Children
The App is intended for adults in employment contexts and is not directed at children.

9. Changes
We may update this policy. Material changes will be reflected by the effective date above.

10. Third-party links/services
mymcd.eu and next.mymcd.eu operate under their own privacy policies.
